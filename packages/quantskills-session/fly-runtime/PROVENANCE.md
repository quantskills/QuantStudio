# Source and integration

Ported from https://github.com/songshuquant/ssquant-fly-agent at
`ecd0632a20fed7655e9f5355b0b952fc694c891d` (codex/fly-autonomous-v2).

Neural, life, readout learning, statistics, scenery and original home assets are
retained. QuantStudio replaces the SSQUANT/SimNow host, authentication, model and
market dependencies. Contest trading creates human-confirmed plans only.
